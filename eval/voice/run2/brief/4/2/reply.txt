Shauna ran two hundred forty page builds with Claude Sonnet 5.5. Half used a standard design rules file, and half used her system. Her system passed all one hundred twenty pages. The standard file passed one hundred eighteen. The bigger difference was consistency. The standard group ended up with thirty button styles and sixteen field styles. Her system produced just seven button styles and four field styles. You can find the full write-up on the case study called Testing a design system on Claude.
