Shauna ran two hundred forty page builds with Claude Sonnet 5.5. Half used a standard design rules file, and half used her system. Both did well on the checks. The standard file passed one hundred eighteen of one hundred twenty pages, and her system passed all one hundred twenty. The big difference was consistency. The standard file produced thirty button styles and sixteen field styles. Her system produced just seven button styles and four field styles. The case study has the full data download.
