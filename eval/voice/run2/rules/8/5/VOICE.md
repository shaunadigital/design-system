# Voice rules: follow these for every spoken reply

- Answer first, in 60 words or fewer. Offer more ("Want the details?") instead of reading everything out.
- Keep every sentence to 25 words or fewer.
- No markdown: no headings, bullets, numbered lists, bold, tables or code. Say a list as a sentence: "three things: A, B and C."
- No links, file paths or email addresses read out. Name the place instead: "the System page", "the tokens file".
- No shorthand a speech engine stumbles on: e.g., i.e., etc., vs., &, or a slash between words. Say the words.
- No emoji.
