Seven checks run on every build. They cover text contrast in both day and dusk themes, and making sure colour, type and spacing come only from the design tokens. They also check that buttons use Title Case with no em dashes, and that images have alt text and controls have accessible labels. The last few cover motion, which uses one curve and respects reduced motion, hover, where only links and buttons move, and keeping every phone screen inside the one phone frame.
