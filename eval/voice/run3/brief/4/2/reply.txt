Shauna ran 240 page builds with Claude, comparing a standard design rules file against her own system. Nearly every page passed either way. The standard file passed 118 of 120. Her system passed all 120. The big difference was consistency. The standard file produced 30 different button styles and 16 field styles. Her system produced just 7 button styles and 4 field styles.
