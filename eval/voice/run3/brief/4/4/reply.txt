Round two was a test of two hundred forty page builds with Claude Sonnet five point five. Half used a standard design rules file, and half used Shauna's design system. Her system passed all one hundred twenty pages. The standard file passed one hundred eighteen. The bigger difference was consistency. The standard file produced thirty different button styles and sixteen different field styles. Her system produced just seven button styles and four field styles. The full write-up is in the case study called Testing a design system on Claude.
