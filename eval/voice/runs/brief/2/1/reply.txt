Seven checks run on every build. First, text contrast has to be at least four point five to one, in both day and dusk themes. Second, colour, type, and spacing must come from the design tokens only. Third, buttons use Title Case, and there are no em dashes. Fourth, images need alt text and controls need accessible labels. Fifth, motion uses one curve and respects reduced motion. Sixth, only links and buttons move on hover. And seventh, every phone screen sits inside the one phone frame.
