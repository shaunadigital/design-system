Shauna ran two hundred forty page builds with Claude, comparing a standard design rules file against her own system. Both did well on passing. The standard one passed one hundred eighteen of one hundred twenty pages, and hers passed all one hundred twenty. The big difference was consistency. The standard file produced thirty different button styles and sixteen different field styles. Her system produced just seven button styles and four field styles. The case study on her site has the full write-up and a data download.
