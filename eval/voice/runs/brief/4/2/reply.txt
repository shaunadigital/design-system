Round two was two hundred forty page builds with Claude Sonnet five point five. Half used the standard design rules file, and half used Shauna's system. Both did well on passing. The standard file passed one hundred eighteen of one hundred twenty pages, and Shauna's system passed all one hundred twenty. The big difference was consistency. The standard file produced thirty different button styles and sixteen field styles. Shauna's system produced just seven button styles and four field styles. The full write-up and data are on the case study page.
