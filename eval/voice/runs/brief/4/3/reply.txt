Shauna ran two hundred forty page builds with Claude, comparing a standard design rules file against her own system. Nearly everything passed either way. The standard file passed one hundred eighteen of one hundred twenty pages, and her system passed all one hundred twenty. The real difference was consistency. The standard file produced thirty different button styles and sixteen field styles. Her system produced just seven button styles and four field styles.
