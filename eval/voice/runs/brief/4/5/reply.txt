Shauna ran two hundred forty page builds with Claude, comparing a standard design file against her own system. Both did well on passing. The standard one passed one hundred eighteen of one hundred twenty pages, and hers passed all one hundred twenty. The big difference was consistency. With the standard file, Claude invented thirty different button styles and sixteen field styles. With her system, it used just seven button styles and four field styles. The full write-up is in the case study on testing a design system on Claude.
